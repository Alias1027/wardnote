## Original-file distribution and use

On 2026-10-25, the author chose to distribute the original V007 PDFs without adding certificate signatures, password encryption or printing restrictions. Public templates can be edited, transcribed or reconstructed, and technical controls cannot fully prevent these activities. This decision does not dismiss the value of digital signatures for verifying origin and integrity. For this distribution, ease of printing, completion and use across devices takes priority, supported by official downloads, clear versions, copyright/license notices and SHA-256 comparisons.

SHA-256 checks compare a download byte-for-byte with the officially distributed original. They do not identify a signer, prevent editing or replace CA identity verification. Obtain checksum references from this repository or its Release. Completed and saved copies will normally have different hashes.

Print on A4 and write by hand, or use text, ink or annotation tools in a compatible PDF reader. No interactive form fields were added in this update; reader support must be checked separately. V007 is a worksheet version independent of the app versions; this publication does not redesign app forms or change clinical functionality. Never upload completed records, patient information, recordings or backups to the public repository or feedback system.
